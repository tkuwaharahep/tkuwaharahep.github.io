02Research Area

Dark Matter Physics

Recent studies focus on Composite/WIMP/Macroscopic Dark Matters.
Phenomenological/Cosmological implications, Detection/Observation Methods (including collider, cosmology, astrophysics), and Theoretical Model Buildings.
Recently focus on composite dark matter models, especially those with a dark QCD sector.

Precision Physics

My works span Flavor Physics and CP violations in beyond the Standard Model.
Proton decay in Supersymmetric Grand Unified Theories. Flavor Observables, Electric Dipole Moments, and Lepton Flavor Violations.
